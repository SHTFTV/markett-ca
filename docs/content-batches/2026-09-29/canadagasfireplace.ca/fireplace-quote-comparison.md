# Comparing gas fireplace quotes: seven questions beyond the flame

Two fireplace quotes can show similar-looking appliances while covering different work. Compare the proposed system and installation scope before comparing totals. Use these questions to make a useful shortlist.

## 1. Are we buying heat, appearance, or both?

Explain which room you want to use and how often. Natural Resources Canada distinguishes decorative gas fireplaces from heating models. Ask each supplier to explain the proposed model’s intended use rather than assuming an attractive flame means equivalent heating performance. [1]

## 2. Is the appliance type right for the opening?

An insert uses an existing fireplace cavity, while a factory-built fireplace or freestanding appliance involves a different installation approach. NRCan’s guide outlines these differences. Ask the installer to confirm the suitability of the existing opening and chimney rather than selecting by visible dimensions alone. [2]

## 3. Which efficiency figure is being compared?

Ask for the model’s Canadian EnerGuide information where available. Gas fireplaces are rated using annual Fireplace Efficiency, or FE. Compare like-for-like information and keep the product literature with the quote; a promotional efficiency number without its test basis is difficult to compare. [3]

## 4. What does the venting work include?

Ask where the proposed vent will terminate and whether an existing vent can actually be reused. Direct-vent appliances bring combustion air from outside, but the complete installation still needs to follow the selected model’s requirements. The installer should assess the route and termination before you approve finishing work. [2]

## 5. What is included around the appliance?

Request separate line items for the appliance, venting, gas work, any electrical work, permits, removal, finishing and disposal. Mark owner-supplied items explicitly. Ask who will coordinate the work and what could change the price after a site visit.

## 6. What happens after installation?

Request an explanation of controls, safe-use instructions, warranty registration and maintenance responsibilities. Confirm who handles service and how to obtain the correct manual. Do not accept an assumed service arrangement that is absent from the written quote.

## 7. What evidence closes the job?

Agree in advance on the documentation you will receive: model and serial details, invoices, commissioning or inspection records as applicable, and warranty terms. Keep the comparison sheet until those deliverables are complete.
